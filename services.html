Our Services

End-to-end data leak protection solutions tailored to your organization's unique security requirements.

🔍

Threat Intelligence

Continuous monitoring of dark web forums, marketplaces, and communication channels for early detection of data leaks and emerging threats. Our intelligence feeds aggregate data from thousands of sources worldwide.

🛡️

Breach Prevention

Proactive defense strategies including vulnerability assessments, penetration testing, security architecture review, and implementation of industry-leading data protection frameworks and controls.

⚡

Incident Response

24/7 incident response team ready to contain, eradicate, and recover from security breaches. Our structured approach minimizes downtime, data loss, and reputational damage.

📋

Compliance Audits

Comprehensive compliance assessments against GDPR, HIPAA, PCI-DSS, SOC 2, and ISO 27001 standards. We identify gaps and provide clear remediation roadmaps.

🔐

Data Encryption

Enterprise-grade encryption solutions for data at rest, in transit, and in use. Implementation of key management systems, PKI infrastructure, and zero-trust architectures.

📊

Risk Assessment

Quantitative and qualitative risk analysis using industry-standard methodologies. We identify, assess, and prioritize risks to your data assets with actionable mitigation strategies.

🔄

Security Monitoring

Real-time security event monitoring and correlation using advanced SIEM platforms. 24/7 security operations center providing alert triage and escalation.

🎓

Security Training

Comprehensive security awareness training programs for employees, covering phishing prevention, data handling best practices, and incident reporting procedures.

🔬

Forensic Analysis

Digital forensic investigations following security incidents. Detailed analysis of attack vectors, malware reverse engineering, and evidence preservation for legal proceedings.

Our team of experienced security professionals will work with you to design a protection strategy that fits your specific needs and budget.
